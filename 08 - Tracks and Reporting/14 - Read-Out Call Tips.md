# Read-Out Call Tips

Presenting your findings to the client. Usually a mix of technical and non-technical stakeholders.

[[08 - Tracks and Reporting/00 - README|Folder index]]

## Preparation

- Know your audience — who's on the call (CISO? CTO? Sysadmins? Board?)
- Have the report open and ready to screen share
- Prepare a 5-slide summary: scope, methodology, key findings, attack narrative, recommendations
- Test your screen share and audio before the call

## Presentation flow

```text
1. Scope recap (2 min)
   "We tested X systems over Y days using Z methodology"

2. Executive summary (5 min)
   Overall risk rating, finding counts, key takeaway

3. Attack narrative (10 min)
   Walk through the attack chain — this is where you demonstrate value
   Show key screenshots, explain business impact

4. Top findings deep dive (15 min)
   Critical and high findings with remediation guidance
   Be prepared for "how do we fix this?" questions

5. Q&A (15 min)
   Technical team will have detailed questions
   Exec team will ask about risk and priority
```

## Do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Speak in business terms for execs | Use jargon without explaining it |
| Acknowledge good security controls | Make the client feel attacked |
| Provide prioritized remediation | Present a wall of 50 findings without guidance |
| Be honest about what you couldn't test | Oversell your findings |
| Offer to answer follow-up questions | Rush through critical findings |
